\begin{abstract}
FL-BSA is a customer-hosted evidence appliance for controlled fair-outcomes simulation. Its selected
v5 path uses a first-party, profile-based tabular generator with two separately fitted branches:
an amplification branch that retains configured outcome/protected strata, and an intrinsic control
that excludes outcome aliases during fitting and applies a declared equal-rate post-label policy.
This paper binds its statements to product tag \ProductReleaseTag{}, commit
\IdentityText{\ProductCommitRaw}, and retained v5.0.8 release evidence. In the exact synthetic
fixture, amplification gender AIR is \GenderAIRAmplification{} with 95\% interval
[\GenderAIRAmplificationLCI{}, \GenderAIRAmplificationUCI{}], below the internal 0.80 screen;
the intrinsic policy control is \GenderAIRIntrinsic{}. AOD and EOD are unavailable, equalized-odds
outputs are not informative because the confusion surface is degenerate, and calibration is not
evaluated. Both branch quality scores exceed the configured 0.80 composite threshold, but that
composite includes a privacy quality heuristic and is neither a formal privacy guarantee nor a
predictive-utility result. All \RobustnessPasses{}/\RobustnessRuns{} configured robustness scenario
contracts passed. The amplification utility diagnostic averages \UtilityMeanAUC{} ROC AUC;
\UtilityBelowFloor{} of 40 runs are below the configured 0.70 floor, so production utility is not
established. The accompanying deterministic ZIP provides the exact intake, a sanitized 40-run
projection, source identities, checksums, and an offline verifier.
\end{abstract}
