Headline AIR confidence intervals use Wilson intervals for selection rates and a delta method for
the log ratio \citep{wilson1927probable,oehlert1992delta}. SRG intervals use conservative
differences of Wilson endpoints. Pairwise race p-values use Holm's procedure
\citep{holm1979sequential}. These are conditional row-surface approximations and omit fixture,
source-population, profile-fitting, seed, version, selection, and deployment uncertainty. The
machine-readable intake also retains the legacy BCa/bootstrap annex configuration (2,000
replicates, seed 42), but the deterministic v4 uncertainty surface is the PDF source of truth.
