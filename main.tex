\begin{abstract}
FL-BSA is a customer-hosted evidence appliance that generates two deliberately different
synthetic branches for fairness review. This paper binds its claims to product tag
\ProductReleaseTag{} at commit \IdentityText{\ProductCommitRaw} and reports the corresponding
release-evidence run. On the generated fixture, the historical and amplification gender disparity
ratios are both about \AmplificationAIR{}, while the intrinsic branch reaches
\IntrinsicAIR{} after an equal-rate post-label policy. The latter is a mechanical control showing
that branch separation operates; it is not a causal counterfactual. Release Gold robustness passed
\RobustnessTotalPasses{}/\RobustnessTotalRuns{} configured scenario runs. A new ten-seed
train-on-synthetic/test-on-held-out-fixture study finds material utility variation: mean ROC AUC
\UtilitySyntheticAUCMean{} versus a real-train fixture baseline of \UtilityBaselineAUC{}. The
paper treats AIR 0.80 as an internal screen rather than a lending-law verdict, states the limits of
single-run inference, reports both branches' quality, and supplies a hash-bound offline companion.
\end{abstract}
