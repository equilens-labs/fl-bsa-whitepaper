
\section{Results}
\subsection{Fairness}
\noindent \Cref{tab:gender_air_slices} is the main scenario readout. The historical fixture reports AIR \GenderAIRHistorical{} for \GenderProtectedGroup{} vs \GenderReferenceGroup{}, below the \AirMin{} internal screen. The amplification (bias-preserving) slice reproduces the same level of disparity at AIR \GenderAIRAmplification{}, showing that the branch preserves the configured positive-control signal. The intrinsic (de-biased) ratio of AIR \GenderAIRIntrinsic{} is a direct consequence of its deterministic equal-rate post-label policy. It does not localize causation or measure remediation efficacy. The result is a positive-control pass for diagnostic detection and descriptive branch separation, not a customer portfolio or legal finding.
