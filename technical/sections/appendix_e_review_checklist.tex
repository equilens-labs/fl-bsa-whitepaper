\section{Evaluator Review Checklist}
\label{app:review-checklist}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Verify the public PDF, companion, sidecar, and manifest against
    \texttt{SHA256SUMS.txt}.
  \item Run the companion's offline verifier and retain its JSON receipt.
  \item Confirm the product tag, product commit, runtime image, evidence run, retained source
    attempt, and current successful attempt match Appendix~\ref{app:evidence}.
  \item Read unavailable and not-informative states before interpreting any fairness result.
  \item Keep the amplification comparison's groups, ratio, interval, threshold, and review status
    together.
  \item Treat intrinsic parity as an equal-rate policy control, not a causal or legal conclusion.
  \item Distinguish quality, robustness, utility, privacy, and scenario-contract dispositions.
  \item Record the 36/40 sub-floor advisory utility result as an open limitation.
  \item Review race suppression and intersectional coverage against the intended population.
  \item Define deployment-specific validation, legal review, security, privacy, retention,
    monitoring, and escalation before any operational use.
\end{enumerate}

A complete technical evaluation should preserve the paper and companion as a pair. Screenshots or
isolated metric extracts lose the availability, provenance, and claim-boundary information needed
to interpret the numbers correctly.
