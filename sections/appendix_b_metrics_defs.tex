\section{Metric and Status Reference}

% TeX Live 2026's tagged-list implementation does not yet emulate enumitem's
% description `style` key. Keep this list on the supported margin/font surface.
\setlist[description]{font=\normalfont\bfseries,leftmargin=0pt}
\begin{description}
  \item[AIR (disparity ratio)] Selection rate of the comparison group divided by the reference
  rate. Undefined if the reference rate is zero. In this paper it is descriptive.
  \item[SRG (selection-rate gap)] Comparison-group selection rate minus reference-group rate.
  Negative values indicate a lower comparison-group rate under the declared ordering. Its reported
  endpoint-difference range is not a calibrated 95\% confidence interval.
  \item[Point relation] Whether the AIR point estimate is below or at/above the internally selected
  0.80 line.
  \item[AIR interval relation] Whether the entire 95\% AIR interval is below, crosses, or is
  entirely above the line. This is reported independently of point relation and is not applied to
  the policy-determined intrinsic row.
  \item[Overall quality] Branch-certificate composite. It does not include every possible quality,
  privacy, fairness, or utility dimension.
  \item[TSTR] Train on synthetic, test on a held-out partition of the original generated fixture.
  The reported study uses equal-sized training tables and excludes protected attributes from the
  downstream model.
  \item[Not evaluated] Required inputs or a declared evaluation were absent; no conclusion follows.
  \item[Not informative] A metric object exists, but the scenario's degenerate cells do not support
  the intended comparison; it is not counted as favorable evidence.
\end{description}
