\section{Statistical Analysis Plan Summary}

\begin{KeyValueList}
\item[Analysis population] Rows present in the generated fixture or selected branch artifact; no
population-weighting or applicant-level causal estimand.
\item[Primary comparison] Female versus male gender selection rate. Race retained as a bounded
multi-group secondary surface.
\item[Selection-rate interval] Two-sided 95\% Wilson score interval without continuity correction.
\item[AIR interval] Delta method on the log selection-rate ratio, then exponentiated.
\item[SRG endpoint range] $[L_g-U_r,U_g-L_r]$, declared as
\path{conservative_wilson_endpoint_difference}; based on separate 95\% Wilson intervals but not
itself a calibrated 95\% confidence interval.
\item[Tests] Two-sided two-proportion $z$ tests; small-count policy can select an exact alternative.
\item[Multiplicity] Holm--Bonferroni for race AIR p-values; displayed confidence intervals are not
multiplicity-adjusted.
\item[Internal AIR line] 0.80, reported as point relation and interval relation; never a legal
verdict.
\item[Race reference] Configured white retained in provenance; active highest-observed-rate policy
selects black for this run.
\item[Display floor] At least \RaceDisplayMinGroupN{} rows and \RaceDisplayMinGroupPct{}\% share for
main-PDF race display. The minimum group has \RaceMinimumGroupN{} rows
(\RaceMinimumGroupPct{}\%): it \RaceCountFloorRelation{} the count condition and
\RaceShareFloorRelation{} the share condition.
\item[Unavailable states] ECE \texttt{NOT\_EVALUATED}; EO \texttt{NOT\_INFORMATIVE}.
\item[Intrinsic inference] Policy-determined approvals; interval and $p$-value fields are retained
for format symmetry only and have no inferential interpretation.
\end{KeyValueList}

The headline uncertainty objects are deterministic for fixed inputs. Bootstrap BCa fields with
2,000 replicates remain in compatibility metadata but are not substituted for the methods above.
