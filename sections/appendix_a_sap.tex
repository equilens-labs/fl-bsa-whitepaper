Primary comparison & Female versus male gender selection rate. Race retained as a bounded
multi-group secondary surface. \\
Selection-rate interval & Two-sided 95\% Wilson score interval without continuity correction. \\

SRG interval & $[L_g-U_r,U_g-L_r]$, declared as

Tests & Two-sided two-proportion $z$ tests; small-count policy can select an exact alternative. \\
Multiplicity & Holm--Bonferroni for race AIR p-values; displayed confidence intervals are not
multiplicity-adjusted. \\
Internal AIR line & 0.80, reported as point relation and interval relation; never a legal verdict. \\
Race reference & Configured white retained in provenance; active highest-observed-rate policy
selects black for this run. \\
Display floor & At least 300 rows and 5\% share for main-PDF race display. The count condition is
met; the share condition is not. \\
Unavailable states & ECE \texttt{NOT\_EVALUATED}; EO \texttt{NOT\_INFORMATIVE}. \\
